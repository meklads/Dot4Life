🌿 Peace Starts at Home

Capsule #1 · June 28, 2026

The Gift of Listening

This week's practice: when your child speaks, stop what you are doing. Turn your body toward them. Make eye contact. Do not interrupt, correct, or solve. Simply listen — fully, quietly, without preparing your response.

Most children do not need more advice. They need more attention. A child who feels heard is a child who feels safe. And a child who feels safe is a child who can grow.

"Peace starts at home. One breath. One pause. One listening moment at a time."
