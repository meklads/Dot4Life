✈️ Best Family Travel Destinations in the Gulf

✈️ Travel📅 June 15, 2026⏱ 8 min read

Family travel is a wonderful opportunity to bond and unwind. The Gulf offers excellent destinations for all family members — from beaches to theme parks.

1. Dubai, UAE

• Dubai Aquarium · Legoland · Motiongate · IMG Worlds.
• Free public beaches: Jumeirah, La Mer, Kite Beach.
• Best time: November-March.

2. Abu Dhabi, UAE

• Ferrari World · Yas Waterworld · Warner Bros World.
• Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque (cultural experience).
• Saadiyat Island (quiet beaches).

3. Jeddah, Saudi Arabia

• Corniche · Beaches · Al-Balad historical area.
• Floating Mosque · Waterfall Park.
• World-class restaurants by the sea.

4. Salalah, Oman

• Khareef season (June-September) — European weather.
• Darbat waterfalls · Jabal Al Qara · Wadi Darbat.
• Best summer destination in the Gulf.

Budget Family Travel Tips

• Book early (2+ months ahead).
• Choose hotels with kitchenettes.
• Use discount cards (Entertainer, coupons).
• Travel in off-peak seasons.
