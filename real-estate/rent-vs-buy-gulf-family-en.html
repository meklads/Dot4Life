🏠 Rent vs Buy for Gulf Families - Which is Better?

🏠 Real Estate📅 June 15, 2026⏱ 7 min read

One of the biggest financial decisions Gulf families face: buy a home or rent? The answer depends on personal and financial factors.

Quick Comparison

FactorRentBuy
Down paymentNone10-20%
FlexibilityHighLow
Monthly costsRent onlyLoan + maintenance
Wealth buildingNoProperty appreciates
MaintenanceLandlordYou

When to Rent

• Early in your career.
• Haven't saved a down payment.
• Uncertain about staying in the city.
• Property prices are too high.

When to Buy

• Stable career.
• Can afford the down payment.
• Planning to stay 5+ years.
• Mortgage payment ≤ rent.
