E-commerce is booming in the Gulf. Amazon UAE, Noon, and local platforms make it possible to start selling with minimal upfront investment. Options include: dropshipping (no inventory needed), Amazon FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon), selling on Instagram and TikTok Shop, and creating your own online store using Shopify or WooCommerce.
