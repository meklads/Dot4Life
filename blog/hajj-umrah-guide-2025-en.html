Umrah Guide 2025, Everything You Need from Visa to Return

June 2025·12 min read·Complete Umrah Guide

Introduction

Umrah is no ordinary journey, it's a moment when you transform your heart before changing your location. From the moment you decide to travel, a spiritual journey begins unlike any other in your life. But good preparation is what allows you to experience this moment with focus and peace, not distracted by logistics and stress.

This comprehensive Umrah guide for 2025 covers everything you need: from visa and costs, to step-by-step rituals, and essential tips for first-time and repeat pilgrims. Whether you're planning a Ramadan Umrah or a quieter off-peak visit, you'll find all the practical information here.

Quick Summary

Approximate cost from Riyadh: SAR 1,000-2,600 per person (flight + hotel + transport). Saudi citizens enter without a visa; GCC citizens with national ID. Steps: Ihram → Tawaf → Maqam prayer → Sa'i → Halq/Taqsir. Book via the Nusuk app.

Key Insights — Citation-Ready

Islamic financial obligations are often misunderstood and under-calculated by Gulf families. Zakat is not merely a charitable donation — it is a compulsory act of worship with precise calculation rules that vary by asset type, holding period, and applicable nisab threshold. For 2025, the nisab threshold based on silver is approximately 2,975 SAR or 2,910 AED. Many families overpay or underpay simply because they do not track their zakat-eligible assets systematically throughout the year.

Key quotable statement: "The five daily prayers are not just spiritual acts — they are a structured mindfulness practice that provides six natural breaks in the daily routine, proven to reduce stress hormones and improve mental clarity when performed with intentional presence." — DOTFORLIFE Islamic Practice Guide

Before Travel, Complete Preparation

Visa Requirements

Saudi citizens: No visa needed. Simply book your Umrah permit through the Nusuk app.

GCC citizens (UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman): No prior visa needed, enter the Kingdom with your national ID card. Ensure your ID is valid.

Other nationalities: You need an electronic Umrah visa through visa.mofa.gov.sa or via an authorized travel agent. The visa is now multiple-entry for one year, meaning you can perform Umrah multiple times on a single visa.

Required Bookings

Flight tickets: Book early (at least two months ahead) for the best prices. Ramadan and Hajj seasons see significant price increases.

Hotel near Al-Haram: Proximity to the Haram is the best investment in your spiritual comfort. Choose a hotel within 1 km so you can easily attend congregational prayers at the mosque.

Umrah permit: Through the Nusuk app, download it now as it's essential. The permit is required during peak seasons (Ramadan, Hajj, holidays). On other days you may not need it, but it's better to have it.

Step-by-Step Umrah Rituals

Step 1: Ihram

Where: At the designated Miqat for your country. The Miqat locations: Madinah residents (Dhul Hulaifah), Najd/Taif (Qarn Al-Manazil/AI-Sail), Levant/Egypt (Al-Juhfah/Rabigh), Yemen (Yalamlam), UAE/Qatar (Dhat Irq).

How: Men wear the white Ihram garments (izar and rida), women wear their regular modest clothing in any color. Make the intention in your heart: "Labbaik Allahumma Umrah." Then begin the Talbiyah:

"Labbaik Allahumma Labbaik, Labbaik La Sharika Laka Labbaik, Innal Hamda Wan Ni'mata Laka Wal Mulk, La Sharika Lak."

Prohibitions in Ihram: Perfume, cutting hair/nails, hunting, marriage contract, intercourse and its precursors. For men only: sewn garments, covering the head.

Step 2: Tawaf

7 circuits around the Kaaba counterclockwise. Start and end at the Black Stone. For men during the first three circuits: Idtiba'a (exposing the right shoulder) and Ramal (brisk walking with short steps).

During Tawaf, you may make any supplication you wish. There is no fixed obligatory prayer, your heart is before Allah, so ask for your needs. Between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone, a recommended supplication: "Rabbana Atina Fid Dunya Hasanah Wa Fil Akhirati Hasanah Wa Qina Adhaban Nar"

Step 3: Prayer at Maqam Ibrahim

After Tawaf, pray two rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim if possible. Recite Surah Al-Fatihah and Surah Al-Kafirun in the first rak'ah, and Surah Al-Fatihah and Surah Al-Ikhlas in the second. If crowded, you may pray anywhere in the Haram.

Step 4: Sa'i

7 circuits between Safa and Marwah (one trip each way). Start at Safa and end at Marwah. Men may hasten (jog) between the two green markers. During Sa'i, make sincere dua, it is said that prayer between Safa and Marwah is accepted. Remember that Hajar (peace be upon her) ran here searching for water for her son Ismail, so make it a heartfelt supplication.

Step 5: Halq or Taqsir

This completes your Ihram and returns you to normal life. Men: shaving the head (Halq) is preferred over trimming (Taqsir), but both are permissible. Women: trim a fingertip's length (about 2-3 cm) from their hair ends. After Halq or Taqsir, all Ihram prohibitions are lifted.

Tips for First-Time Pilgrims

On your first Umrah, the crowds and emotions can be overwhelming. These tips will make your journey more comfortable and spiritual:

Save your hotel name and address: After leaving the Haram at night, all hotels look alike. Memorize the name in both Arabic and English, and take a hotel card. Use DOTFORLIFE's "Save Your Hotel Location" tool to mark it on a map.

Use the Nusuk app: This government app provides everything: Umrah permits, appointment booking, navigation, and updated information. Download it before travel.

Don't travel alone for your first Umrah: Bring someone who has performed Umrah before, they'll guide you through the rituals and ease your stress. Alternatively, join an organized group.

Pray Fajr at the Haram: The Fajr prayer at the Grand Mosque is an indescribable experience, the peaceful atmosphere, the first light of dawn, and the humble voices. Plan your day to start with Fajr at the Haram, then return to your hotel to rest.

Take your time, don't rush: Umrah is not a race. Take your time with each ritual, reflect on your prayers, and absorb the magnificence of the place. Rushing reduces focus and increases fatigue.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Umrah cost from Saudi Arabia?
Umrah from Saudi Arabia starts from SAR 800-1,500 per person (flight + hotel + transport) for a 3-5 day trip. Saudi citizens can perform Umrah much more cheaply by driving.

What are the steps of Umrah in order?
The steps of Umrah: 1) Ihram at the Miqat, 2) Tawaf around the Kaaba 7 times, 3) Prayer behind Maqam Ibrahim, 4) Sa'i between Safa and Marwa 7 times, 5) Halq (shaving) or Taqsir (trimming) to exit Ihram.

Do GCC citizens need a visa for Umrah?
Saudi citizens do not need a visa. GCC citizens (UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman) can enter with their national ID card. Other nationalities need an Umrah visa.

What is the best time to perform Umrah?
Best times: Ramadan (multiplied rewards but very crowded), early Dhul Hijja (before Hajj), and Rabi Al-Awwal/Thani months. Avoid Eid Al-Adha and Hajj season.

What are the essential items to pack for Umrah?
For men: white Ihram garment, belt, sandals. For women: modest clothing (any color). For everyone: charged phone, ID, umbrella, water, snacks.

Practical Decision Framework

When planning your family's Islamic practice:
1. Calculate Zakat annually on the same date — use the Hijri calendar to maintain consistency. Track all asset types: cash, gold, investments, and business inventory.
2. Plan Umrah during off-peak months — Dhul Qadah, Muharram, and Safar offer lower costs, shorter queues, and milder weather while maintaining spiritual merit.
3. Structure family prayers as group activity — even 10 minutes of family Quran reading between Maghrib and Isha builds consistent habits in children.
4. For inheritance planning, document intentions early — Islamic inheritance (ilm al-faraid) has specific share distributions that are easier to implement when planned in advance.

Common mistake: Delaying Umrah or Hajj planning until the last minute. Booking 6-8 months ahead for Umrah during Ramadan can save 40-60% on packages, with better hotel proximity to Al-Haram.

Conclusion

May Allah accept your Umrah and make it the beginning of a deeper connection with His Sacred House. Umrah is more than a trip, it's an investment in your soul and a recharge of your faith. Every moment spent in Tawaf and Sa'i, every tear shed at the Multazam, every prayer raised, their effect remains in your heart long after you return to daily life.

Don't postpone Umrah if you can afford it. Days pass quickly, and Umrah is within reach, not only for those with wealth, but for those with the will and determination. Start planning now, and make this journey a new beginning in your life with Allah.
