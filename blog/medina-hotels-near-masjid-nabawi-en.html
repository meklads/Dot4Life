Medina Hotels Near Masjid an-Nabawi: Complete Guide

When planning your visit to the Prophet's Mosque, choosing the right hotel is one of the most important decisions affecting your trip comfort. Being close to Masjid an-Nabawi means easy access for prayers and visitation, especially for seniors and families with children. This complete guide covers the best hotels near the Prophet's Mosque in Medina, categorized by class and budget, helping you choose the perfect accommodation.

Central Area Around the Haram

Hotels near Masjid an-Nabawi fall into three categories: Zone A (directly on the Haram), Zone B (2-5 minutes walk), and Zone C (5-10 minutes walk). Naturally, closer hotels cost more, but the time and energy saved is worth it, especially during peak seasons.

5-Star Hotels Near the Prophet's Mosque

Marriott Executive Apartments

Located in the central area, just 30 seconds from King Abdulaziz Gate. Features luxurious suites with direct Masjid an-Nabawi views. Multiple restaurants, gym, parking, and laundry service. Price range: 800-1500 SAR/night in regular season. Excellent for families with large suites.

Sheraton Madinah

One of the oldest and most famous hotels in the central area, one minute from the Haram via a direct bridge. Large rooms, diverse restaurants, pool, and health center. Price range: 700-1200 SAR/night. Suitable for both families and business travelers.

Mövenpick Madinah

Located 3 minutes from the Haram on Abu Ayyub Al-Ansari Street. Modern design, excellent service with international buffet. Price range: 600-1000 SAR/night.

Mercure Madinah

Located 2 minutes from the Haram, opposite Marriott. Great value for its category, clean modern rooms. Price range: 500-700 SAR/night.

Dar Al Iman

5-star hotel on the south side of the Haram, 3 minutes walk from Bab al-Rahmah. Competitive rates. Price range: 500-800 SAR/night.

4-Star Hotels Near the Haram

Dar Al Taqwa

One minute from the Haram, excellent service at reasonable prices. Family rooms and suites available. Price range: 400-600 SAR/night.

Qubat Al Madinah

Located 5 minutes from the Haram. Good budget option with helpful staff. Price range: 300-450 SAR/night.

Al Eiman Royal

3 minutes from the Haram, affordable rates. Price range: 350-500 SAR/night.

Budget Hotels Near the Haram

For budget travelers, hotels 5-10 minutes from the Haram include Al Eiman Oasis (7 min), Al Andalus Palace (5 min), and Taibah Hotel (8 min). Prices: 150-350 SAR/night.

Mid-Range Hotels (10-15 minutes)

Holiday Inn Madinah, Danat Al Madinah, and Novotel Madinah are 10-15 minutes away. Prices 200-400 SAR/night with free shuttle buses to the Haram during prayer times.

Budget Comparison

Under 300 SAR: Al Eiman Oasis or Taibah (5-10 min walk). 300-500 SAR: Qubat Al Madinah or Dar Al Taqwa (3-5 min). 500-800 SAR: Dar Al Iman or Mercure (2-3 min). 800+ SAR: Marriott or Sheraton (under 1 min). Book early, especially for Ramadan and Hajj seasons.

Booking Tips

Book through trusted platforms or directly with the hotel for best rates. Read guest reviews on cleanliness and service. Request higher floors for Haram views. Check cancellation policies. For families, ask about connecting rooms or suites. Inquire about shuttle service if staying further away.

Best Areas to Stay in Medina

Medina is organized around the central area near Masjid an-Nabawi. The best areas are the northern zone (King Abdulaziz Gate, King Fahd Gate) with luxury hotels, the southern zone (Bab al-Rahmah) with budget and mid-range options, and the western zone (Abu Ayyub al-Ansari Street) with a mix of hotels. The northern zone is closest to the Haram and provides easy access to the Rawdah. The southern zone is near traditional markets and shopping. The western zone is close to parking areas and bus stops.

When choosing an area, consider: proximity to your preferred Haram gate, availability of restaurants and cafes, access to transportation, and noise levels. The northern zone is the quietest and closest, while the southern zone is more lively with affordable local restaurants.

Hotels with Haram View

A room overlooking Masjid an-Nabawi and the Green Dome is a dream for every visitor. Hotels offering direct Haram views include: Marriott Executive Apartments, Sheraton Madinah, Dar Al Taqwa, and some Mövenpick rooms. Rooms with views cost 30-50% more than interior rooms. Best floors for views are from the 7th to 15th floor.

Tip: For a great view at a reasonable price, request mid-level floors (7-10) at Dar Al Taqwa, offering excellent Green Dome views at lower rates than Marriott or Sheraton.

Family-Friendly Hotels

For families traveling with children, we recommend hotels with: connecting rooms or family suites (Marriott, Dar Al Taqwa, Dar Al Iman), extreme proximity to the Haram to minimize walking with kids (Marriott, Sheraton, Dar Al Taqwa), restaurants with children's menus, laundry service, and children's play areas if available. Marriott Executive Apartments is the best family choice with two-bedroom suites, a living room, and a small kitchenette. Dar Al Taqwa is a great budget-friendly family option.

Hotels for Seniors and People with Disabilities

Seniors need hotels very close to the Haram (under 1 minute walk). Best options: Marriott (direct bridge), Sheraton (direct bridge), Dar Al Taqwa (1 minute). Ensure the hotel offers: low-floor rooms, accessible bathrooms, free shuttle carts to the Haram, wheelchairs on request. Marriott and Sheraton are ideal with direct covered bridges that eliminate street walking.

Seasonal Price Comparison

Hotel prices vary dramatically by season. In regular seasons (outside Umrah and Hajj), prices are 40-60% lower. During Umrah season (Sha'ban, Ramadan, Shawwal), prices can double. During Hajj (Dhul Hijjah), hotels fill up weeks in advance. Best travel times for price and crowds: Muharram, Safar, and Rajab. Book early for better rates. Use comparison sites like Booking, Al Musafir, and Travel Ylla.

Additional Services Offered by Medina Hotels

Medina hotels compete in offering distinguished services. Key services to look for when booking: luggage assistance, parking (free or paid in hotels outside the central zone), laundry and dry cleaning (essential for families staying over a week), 24-hour front desk, and airport shuttle services. Some luxury hotels offer concierge services to help organize tours and restaurant bookings. Marriott and Sheraton offer VIP reception at the airport for premium guests.

For dining, most central area hotels offer daily breakfast buffets featuring Arabic and international cuisine. Some hotels provide 24-hour room service - very useful for families who prefer dining in their rooms. Mövenpick is famous for its chocolate café, while Marriott features an excellent Lebanese restaurant. For those seeking local cuisine, we recommend trying restaurants in the southern zone outside the hotels.

Top Tips for a Comfortable Stay

Request a room on higher floors to reduce noise from the street and crowds. If you're a light sleeper, ask for a room facing away from the Haram (interior courtyard). Bring earplugs during peak seasons. Check the hotel's prayer room schedule - many hotels have dedicated prayer rooms for men and women. For extended stays (over 5 nights), ask about weekly rates which can be 20-30% cheaper than nightly rates.

For families with infants, ask about crib availability at booking time - most 5-star and 4-star hotels provide them free of charge. Also check if the hotel has a mini-fridge in the room for storing baby milk and food. Some hotels like Marriott and Sheraton provide microwave ovens upon request for warming baby food. In-room dining menus often include children's portions at reduced prices.

FAQs

Best family hotel near Masjid an-Nabawi?

Marriott Executive Apartments offers the best family suites, followed by Dar Al Taqwa for its proximity and reasonable prices.

Are there cheap hotels near the Haram?

Yes, Al Eiman Oasis, Taibah, and Al Andalus Palace offer rooms from 150 SAR/night, 5-10 minutes walk away.

Do hotels provide free shuttle to the Haram?

Some mid-range hotels offer free shuttle buses during prayer times. Check with the hotel before booking.

Best time to book a hotel in Medina?

Book 2-3 months before your trip for best rates. Prices rise significantly during Ramadan and Hajj.

Can I get a room with Haram view?

Marriott, Sheraton, and Dar Al Taqwa offer rooms with direct Masjid an-Nabawi views. Request these at booking.
