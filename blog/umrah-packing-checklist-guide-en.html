Umrah Packing Checklist Guide

2026-06-088 min read

Introduction

Packing for Umrah is different from any other trip. You need to balance religious requirements, comfort in extreme heat, and the practical needs of your family — all while keeping your luggage manageable. A forgotten essential can mean unnecessary stress or expense during your spiritual journey.

This comprehensive checklist covers everything your family needs for a smooth and comfortable Umrah trip. Use our interactive Umrah Packing Checklist tool to track your packing progress.

Packing Principle

Pack light but smart. For a 7-10 day Umrah trip, one medium suitcase per person plus a small backpack for daily use is sufficient. Layering is key for changing temperatures between Makkah and Madinah.

Ihram and Prayer Essentials

For men, the ihram consists of two white seamless cloths. Bring at least two sets — one for wearing and one spare. For women, there is no special ihram garment; modest clothing that covers the full body (excluding hands and face) is worn. Essentials include:

  • 2-3 sets of ihram cloth for men (pre-washed and ironed)
  • 1-2 pairs of comfortable sandals or flip-flops (men in ihram cannot wear stitched footwear)
  • Modest, loose-fitting abayas or dresses for women (lightweight fabrics recommended)
  • Prayer mat (lightweight, travel-sized)
  • Quran and dua book (or use phone apps)
  • Tasbih (prayer beads) or digital counter
  • Small bag for shoes when entering the Haram

Clothing for the Family

The climate in Makkah and Madinah is hot year-round, but air-conditioned spaces can be cold. Pack accordingly:

  • Lightweight, breathable fabrics: Cotton and linen are ideal. Avoid synthetic materials that trap heat.
  • Men: Thobes (3-4 for the trip), casual trousers, and short-sleeve shirts for non-prayer times
  • Women: Abayas (2-3), light dresses, long skirts, and loose tops. Bring a light cardigan for cold air conditioning.
  • Children: Light cotton clothing, extra sets for frequent changes, sun hats, and light jackets for evening
  • Undergarments: Enough for the trip plus 2 extra sets
  • Scarves or shawls for women and girls

Footwear

You will walk extensively — often 5-15 km daily within the Haram area. Good footwear is essential:

  • Comfortable walking sandals or sports sandals (primary footwear)
  • Slip-on shoes for entering the Haram (you remove shoes at the door)
  • Light socks (3-4 pairs, moisture-wicking)
  • For men in ihram: open-toed sandals that do not cover the ankle bone

Toiletries and Health Items

Don't assume you can easily find specific brands. Pack your essentials:

  • Unscented soap and unscented shampoo (perfumed products are not allowed in ihram)
  • Miswak or toothpaste and brush
  • Deodorant (unscented or very mild fragrance)
  • Sunscreen (high SPF, sweat-resistant)
  • Lip balm with SPF
  • Hand sanitizer and wet wipes
  • Tissues and small pack of toilet paper
  • Nail clippers and small scissors

Medications and First Aid

Pharmacies are available but having your own supplies saves time and stress:

  • Prescription medications: Enough for the trip plus 3-5 extra days. Carry prescriptions.
  • Pain relievers: Paracetamol, ibuprofen
  • Digestive aids: Antacids, rehydration salts, anti-diarrhea medication
  • Cold and flu remedies: Especially for children
  • Band-aids, antiseptic cream, blister pads
  • Motion sickness medication if needed for flights or bus rides
  • Electrolyte powder or tablets for hydration

    • Passports (valid for at least 6 months) and copies
    • Umrah visa documentation
    • Flight tickets and hotel confirmations (digital + printed)
    • Health insurance documents
    • Cash in Saudi Riyals (small denominations for daily expenses)
    • Credit/debit cards (inform your bank of travel)

    Electronics and Gadgets

    • Smartphone with Quran and prayer time apps installed
    • Power bank (10,000 mAh or larger — essential for long days at the Haram)
    • Chargers and cables (USB-C and Lightning as needed)
    • Saudi power adapter (Type G, same as UK)
    • Earphones for calls and Quran listening
    • Digital watch or fitness tracker for step counting

    Items for Children

    Traveling with children requires additional preparation:

    • Light stroller (compact, easy to fold — essential for walking distances)
    • Baby carrier for crowded areas where strollers cannot pass
    • Diapers and wipes (enough for the trip + emergency supply)
    • Baby formula and bottles (bring more than you think you need)
    • Small toys, coloring books, or tablet for quiet entertainment
    • Snacks (crackers, biscuits, juice boxes)
    • Extra clothing changes (children get dirty quickly)
    • Children's medications (fever reducer, teething gel, etc.)

    What NOT to Pack

    • Perfumed products (not allowed in ihram for both men and women)
    • Stitched clothing for men in ihram (underwear, shirts, trousers)
    • Valuables and excessive jewelry
    • Large, heavy suitcases (you will regret them in crowded transport)
    • Sharp objects in carry-on luggage
    • Non-Islamic literature or items

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What are the most important items to pack for Umrah?
    Ihram cloth (men), modest clothing (women), comfortable sandals, prayer mat, unscented toiletries, medications, power bank, and sun protection. See the full list above.

    Can I bring a stroller for my child to the Haram?
    Yes, lightweight strollers are allowed and recommended for young children. However, they can be difficult to use during crowded times. A baby carrier is a helpful backup.

    What should men avoid wearing during ihram?
    Men cannot wear any stitched clothing including underwear, shirts, trousers, and socks. Only two seamless white cloths and open sandals are permitted.

    Is perfume allowed during Umrah?
    No. Men and women in ihram must avoid perfumed products including scented soap, deodorant, and cosmetics. Use unscented alternatives.

    How many sets of clothing should I pack for a 10-day Umrah trip?
    3-4 thobes for men, 2-3 abayas for women, and 5-7 undergarments per person. Choose lightweight fabrics that are easy to wash and dry.
