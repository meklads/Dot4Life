Umrah Packing Checklist Guide

2026-06-088 min read

Umm Khalid Story: Turning Travel Chaos into an Organized Umrah Journey

"Every time I travel for Umrah, I forget something important." This is how Umm Khalid, a mother of four from Jeddah in her early forties, describes her experience before she discovered an organized packing system. On her first Umrah trip, she left her ATM card at home. On the second, she left her father blood pressure medication at the hotel. On the third, she bought supplies in Mecca at double the price because she had not brought what she needed. Last year, Umm Khalid decided to prepare a comprehensive packing list three weeks before her trip, started packing a week before departure, and organized everything in separate categorized bags. The result was a comfortable, stress-free Umrah journey where she saved over 800 SAR because she did not have to buy missing items in Mecca at inflated prices.

Her story repeats with thousands of Gulf families who travel for Umrah every year. According to the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah, over 8 million pilgrims visit the Kingdom annually, and many face organizational challenges that could be avoided with a good packing list and advance preparation. The difference between an organized journey and a chaotic one is a comprehensive packing list that you start preparing well before your travel date. Umm Khalid now shares her list with friends and family, and every one of them has thanked her for saving them from the stress of forgotten essentials.

Before Travel: Essential Umrah Preparations

Good Umrah preparation starts three to four weeks before your travel date, not two days before as many people do. The first and most important step is checking your passport validity: it must have at least 6 months of validity remaining from your travel date. Second, obtaining your Umrah visa electronically through the "Nusuk" platform (formerly "Eatmarna") or through an authorized travel agent, and booking your Umrah appointment in the app before travel. Third, booking suitable accommodation: choosing a hotel close to the Haram reduces exhaustion, especially for the elderly and people with special needs. Fourth, packing a first aid kit and prescription medications in sufficient quantity for the entire trip, because some medications may not be readily available in Mecca or Medina. Fifth, notifying your bank about your travel to avoid having your credit cards blocked when used in Saudi Arabia if you are coming from abroad. Sixth, downloading essential apps such as "Nusuk" for managing Umrah permits and prayers at the Rawdah, "Haramin Maps" for navigation within the two Holy Mosques, and your airline app for flight updates. Seventh, arranging transportation to and from the airport, whether using a private car, a ride-hailing app, or pre-booking parking. Eighth, preparing a small carry-on bag containing important documents, medications, money, phone, and charger, so it is always with you and not in the main luggage that could be lost or delayed.

Comprehensive Umrah Packing List: Everything You Need

To avoid forgetting anything important, here is a comprehensive packing list categorized by type of item. This list covers the needs of men, women, and children on a typical Umrah trip lasting 7 to 14 days:

Documents and Money: Passport and a copy, printed Umrah visa, confirmed hotel booking, flight tickets, electronic boarding pass, national ID card, ATM and credit cards, sufficient cash in Saudi Riyals, a secure money wallet, and a copy of your travel health insurance documents.

Men Clothing: At least two Ihram sets (two or three for comfort), regular clothes for moving around (3-4 outfits), a light jacket or sweater (the Haram is heavily air-conditioned), pajamas for sleeping, comfortable walking shoes (the most important item), slippers for the hotel and wudu, ghutra or shemagh and agal, and enough underwear for 7-10 days.

Women Clothing: Light abayas (2-3), comfortable long dresses or skirts, a light jacket or bisht, prayer hijabs (4-6 pieces), a light shayla, comfortable flat walking shoes, comfortable slippers, enough underwear, and a small handbag for the Haram.

Health and Hygiene: Toothbrush and toothpaste, travel-size shampoo and conditioner, soap and antiseptic, deodorant, alcohol-free perfume (for Ihram), wet wipes and tissues, skin and lip moisturizer (the Haram air is dry), sunscreen, shaving kit, comb and hairbrush, hand sanitizer, and extra face masks.

First Aid and Medications: Small first aid kit (bandages, antiseptic, pain reliever, gauze, small scissors, thermometer), chronic medications in double quantity, headache, cold and allergy medications, stomach and constipation medication (common during travel), motion sickness medication, immune-boosting vitamins, and muscle and joint pain cream.

Electronics: Mobile phone with charger and backup cable, large capacity power bank, earphones, smartwatch for step tracking, power adapter (if coming from outside Saudi Arabia), and a small camera.

Other Items: Small backpack for daily carrying at the Haram, reusable water bottle (for Zamzam water), light prayer mat, prayer beads, small Quran or Quran app, nail clippers, plastic bags for dirty clothes, small travel pillow for the plane, small luggage lock, and a notebook and pen.

Smart Packing Tips for Your Umrah Trip

How you organize your luggage is just as important as what is inside it. Follow these smart packing tips for better space management and organization. First, use packing cubes to separate different types of clothing and items. These cubes are available at travel stores starting from 30 SAR and make a huge difference in organization. Second, roll your clothes instead of folding them to save space and prevent wrinkles. Third, place liquids in sealed bags inside a separate pouch to prevent leaks on your clothes. Fourth, distribute valuable items between your main luggage and carry-on bag to minimize loss if one is lost. Fifth, bring healthy snacks (nuts, dates, protein bars) because you may not find healthy options easily during travel or in crowded areas. Sixth, place a copy of your packing list inside the bag so you remember what you have when returning. Seventh, keep a digital copy of all documents in your email or cloud storage as a backup.

Health and Safety Tips During Umrah at the Holy Mosques

Performing Umrah in the Gulf high heat requires special health attention, especially for those performing Umrah in summer months when temperatures in Mecca can reach 45 degrees Celsius or more. The Grand Mosque can be extremely crowded, especially during Ramadan nights and the first weeks of Umrah season, and the crowds can cause severe exhaustion and breathing difficulties. Here are essential health tips for a safe journey: drink water continuously, at least one cup every hour, and use a reusable bottle to refill from the Zamzam water available for free at the mosque. Carry hand sanitizer and extra face masks, and use them especially in crowded areas, as crowding increases the spread of seasonal respiratory infections. Use sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher, and a cap or light umbrella for moving between the hotel and the mosque during peak sun hours. Take regular rest breaks between rituals, and do not exhaust yourself during Sai between Safa and Marwah if you feel tired, as Sai can be performed using the wheelchair available at the mosque for the elderly and people with special needs. If you have chronic conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or asthma, consult your doctor at least three weeks before travel to assess your condition and adjust medications if needed, and make sure to carry your medications in your carry-on bag, not your checked luggage. Keep emergency number 997 in your phone for ambulance services within the mosque, and identify the nearest health center or hospital to your hotel. Additional health guidance from the Saudi Ministry of Health for Umrah helps you maintain your health during the rituals, and remember that your physical health is a trust, and worship can only be performed optimally with a healthy body.

Final Tips Before Closing Your Luggage

Before closing your luggage and heading to the airport, here is a final checklist that will put your mind at ease. First, make sure your passport is in your pocket or carry-on bag, not in the main luggage. Second, take photos of all your documents and email them to yourself as a backup. Third, ensure your phone and power bank are fully charged before heading to the airport. Fourth, inform a family member or friend of your travel date and accommodation details in Mecca and Medina. Fifth, if you have children, prepare a small bag for them with light toys, snacks, and coloring books to pass waiting time at the airport and on the plane. Sixth, do not forget your light prayer mat and small Quran, they will be your companions on this spiritual journey. Seventh, take a deep breath and prepare mentally for a spiritual journey, good organization brings peace of mind and prepares you for focus and devotion in worship. Remember that the primary goal of Umrah is to draw closer to God, and all these material preparations are merely means to achieve this goal with comfort and tranquility. Use the Nusuk platform for all official Umrah permits and planning.

Useful Apps for an Organized Umrah Journey

Technology makes the Umrah journey much easier. The official "Nusuk" app from the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah is the most important app for managing Umrah permits and prayers at the Rawdah. "Haramin Maps" helps you navigate within the Grand Mosque and the Prophet Mosque. Your airline app for flight updates and changes. A Quran app to benefit from waiting time. A currency converter to track exchange rates. A weather app for Mecca and Medina. And most importantly, try to minimize phone usage while inside the mosque to focus on worship and devotion. The Nusuk platform is mandatory for obtaining Umrah permits, so make sure to download it and activate your account well before travel.

Frequently Asked Questions About Umrah Packing

What essential documents must I bring for Umrah?

A valid passport with at least 6 months remaining validity, a printed electronic Umrah visa, hotel booking confirmation, flight tickets, national ID card, ATM and credit cards, and travel health insurance documents. Keep digital copies in your email as backup.

How many bags do I need for a two-week Umrah trip?

One large main suitcase for men, two for women if needed. Plus a small carry-on bag containing valuable documents, medications, electronics, and money. Take half of what you plan to bring, you will find you do not need most of it.

Do I need special shoes for Umrah?

Yes, comfortable walking shoes are the most important item in your bag. You will walk a lot in the Haram, between the hotel and mosque, and during Sai. Choose lightweight, breathable athletic shoes that you have worn before to avoid blisters. Never wear new shoes for the first time during Umrah.

How do I manage chronic medications during Umrah?

Carry your medications in your carry-on bag, not in checked luggage that could be lost or delayed. Bring double the quantity as a reserve. Keep a prescription in both English and Arabic. Consult your doctor before travel to adjust dosages according to ritual timing. Set phone alarms to remind you of medication times.

Which apps should I download before Umrah travel?

Nusuk (mandatory for permits), Haramin Maps, your airline app, a Quran app, currency converter, and weather app for Mecca and Medina. Download these apps before travel and set up your accounts in advance to avoid internet connectivity issues after arrival.
