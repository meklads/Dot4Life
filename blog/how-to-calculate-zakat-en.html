How to Calculate Zakat in Saudi Riyals

November 11, 2026 11 min read

The short answer: zakat is usually 2.5% of your net wealth once it reaches the nisab and a lunar year has passed, and the nisab is measured by the value of 85 grams of gold or 595 grams of silver. At today's prices the gold nisab is about SAR 41,600 and the silver nisab about SAR 3,800, and that huge gap is behind most calculation mistakes.

Picture Abu Fahd in Jeddah, sitting down one night in Ramadan to work out his zakat as he does every year. He pulls out an old note from two years ago that says the nisab is around twenty thousand riyals, and builds his sum on it. He hasn't noticed that the price of a gram of gold has climbed sharply since then, so his old figure is now far from reality. Plenty of people calculate with good intentions and out-of-date numbers. This guide gives you the method in riyals, at this week's prices, with examples you can apply to your own money straight away.

The whole formula in one line

Before any detail, here is the entire idea: add up the wealth that counts for zakat, subtract what can be deducted, then compare the result with the nisab. If it reaches the nisab and a lunar year has passed, zakat is one fortieth, or 2.5% of the amount.

In numbers, one fortieth means SAR 25 for every SAR 1,000, and SAR 2,500 for every SAR 100,000. That rate is fixed and does not move with prices. What changes every day is the nisab itself, because it is tied to the price of gold and silver.

Many people miss this point: the nisab is not a riyal figure written in the books of fiqh. It is a fixed weight of metal. The classical figures are 85 grams of gold or 595 grams of silver, as Musaffa Academy's explainer on the two nisab standards sets out, and you convert them to riyals at the price on your zakat day.

The nisab in riyals this week

A gram of 24K gold in Saudi Arabia was priced at around SAR 489 on 11 November 2026, according to Al Ain's daily gold price report. Silver traded near $52.78 an ounce the same day, according to Fortune, or roughly SAR 6.36 a gram at the fixed rate of 3.75 riyals to the dollar.

Look at the size of that gap: the gold nisab is more than ten times the silver nisab. Scholars hold well-known, different views on which standard to use for cash, and we won't pick between them here. What matters in practice is knowing which one you follow, then calculating it at today's price rather than an old one.

Five steps to work out your zakat

  1. Fix your annual date. Many families choose one date they will remember, such as a set day in Ramadan, and calculate on it every year. Put it in your phone calendar.
  2. Add up your cash on that day. Bank balances, cash at home, deposits and savings. Take each balance as it stands on that day.
  3. Add gold and silver after converting the karat. Multiply the weight of gold by its purity, so 21K is 21 out of 24 and 18K is 18 out of 24, then multiply the result by the price of a gram of 24K gold.
  4. Add the other assets you pay zakat on, such as shares, trade goods and debts you expect to collect, according to the method you follow after asking a scholar.
  5. Subtract what is deducted and compare with the nisab. If the net reaches the nisab, multiply it by 0.025 to find your zakat.

One small habit saves a lot of confusion: keep your figures on a single sheet or in the calculator, and hold on to them. Next year you can compare at a glance and see what changed in your wealth and why your zakat moved.

These examples are illustrative, use prices from 11 November 2026, and assume the person pays zakat on this wealth in the way shown. The point is to see the method, not to copy the numbers.

The third case is the one people ask about most. Thirty thousand riyals is well above the silver nisab but below the gold nisab at today's price. So the answer depends on the standard you follow, and that is exactly the question worth putting to a scholar rather than choosing whatever feels easier.

Notice in the second example that the gold bars were valued as weight times price per gram, 40 × 489. If the gold were 21K, you would convert it first: 40 × 21 ÷ 24 = 35 grams of pure gold, then multiply by the price.

Questions where scholars differ: ask before you calculate

Zakat has clear areas that everyone agrees on, and areas with detail and well-known differences of opinion. We are a site of tools and general information. We do not issue rulings or choose between views, but we want you to know where you stand before you calculate:

  • Gold jewelry in personal use: scholars hold different views, and the calculator lets you enter whatever you want included.
  • Shares and funds: the method differs for long-term investors and active traders, with several approaches.
  • Debts: money owed to you and money you owe, especially long loans such as home finance.
  • Salary and income received during the year: how it joins the rest of your wealth and when its year begins.
  • Which nisab standard to use: gold or silver for cash.

If your wealth includes any of these, write your question down clearly, put it to a scholar you trust or an official fatwa body, then come back to the calculator and enter the figures based on the answer. When should you calculate?

Zakat runs on the lunar year, which is about eleven days shorter than the Gregorian year, roughly 354 days. Anyone who calculates on the same Gregorian date each year slips a little later every time, and the gap builds up.

The practical answer many families use is a fixed Hijri date that is easy to remember, and Ramadan is the most popular choice because it is already a season of giving. Ramadan 1448 is expected in February 2027, so if that is your date, start gathering your figures calmly now rather than in a single night. For the rulings on when your zakat year begins, ask a scholar.

Where to pay zakat in Saudi Arabia

ZATCA encourages individuals to pay their zakat on an optional basis through the Zakaty platform, via the app or the official website, with payment through SADAD, cards and Apple Pay, as set out in ZATCA's announcement on Zakaty. The platform also offers a calculator and reminders.

For zakat al-fitr at the end of Ramadan, the national charity platform Ehsan runs a dedicated service that handles delivery, and it operates under a supervisory committee of 13 government bodies, as Ajel reported. If you personally know people in need among your relatives or neighbours, giving directly is an option you can choose based on what you are comfortable with.

A practical warning: do not deal with accounts or links collecting zakat through anonymous messages or unverified social media profiles. Official platforms use gov.sa addresses or are clearly identified.

The practical takeaway

At heart, zakat is a simple sum: your net wealth, a nisab at today's price, and one fortieth. The real difficulty lies in two things, old numbers and questions where scholars differ. The first is solved by a calculator with an up-to-date price, the second by a clear question to a scholar you trust.

Frequently asked questions

What is the nisab in Saudi riyals today?

At prices on 11 November 2026, the gold nisab (85 grams) is about SAR 41,600 and the silver nisab (595 grams) about SAR 3,800. The value moves every day with metal prices, so work it out at the price on your zakat day or use a zakat calculator that updates the price for you.

How do I convert 21K gold to its 24K equivalent?

Multiply the weight by 21 and divide by 24. So 100 grams of 21K gold equals 87.5 grams of pure gold, which you then multiply by the price of a gram of 24K gold.

Which price should I use, buying or selling?

Calculators usually use the published market price per gram on the calculation day, which is closer to the selling price than to the price of jewelry with workmanship. For valuation details in your case, ask a scholar.

Where can I pay zakat online in Saudi Arabia?

ZATCA offers the Zakaty platform for individuals on an optional basis, through the app or the website, with payment by SADAD, cards and Apple Pay. For zakat al-fitr, the national Ehsan platform runs a dedicated service.

Why are the gold and silver nisab so far apart?

The two weights have been fixed for centuries, but gold has risen many times over against silver, so the gold nisab in riyals is now far higher. Scholars hold different views on which to use for cash, so choose the one you follow after asking a scholar.

Can the calculator remind me of my next zakat date?

Yes. The site's zakat calculator saves your last calculation on your device, shows the next lunar-year date, and can notify you if you allow notifications.
