    Section 8: Scholarships and Financial Aid

    Many international schools and universities in the Gulf offer scholarships based on academic merit, sports achievement, or financial need. Research scholarship opportunities at your target schools. In Saudi Arabia, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Scholarship Program funds citizens to study abroad at top universities. In the UAE, various government and private scholarships are available. Build scholarship applications into your timeline and encourage your children to excel academically.
