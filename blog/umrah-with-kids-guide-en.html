Complete Umrah with Kids Guide: Planning, Tips & Family Travel

2026-06-208 min read

Umrah with Kids: A Spiritual Family Journey

Performing Umrah with your children is more than just a religious journey, it's an investment in raising your children with love for the Holy Mosque and the Prophet's Mosque. Many families in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf region seek the best ways to perform Umrah with their kids, but often face challenges in planning and organizing the trip. This comprehensive guide covers everything you need for a safe, memorable, and spiritually rewarding family Umrah experience.

Performing Umrah with young children requires different preparation than Umrah for adults. Kids need special attention, a flexible schedule, and realistic expectations. However, with proper planning, Umrah can become a transformative educational and spiritual experience that stays in your children's memory for a lifetime.

Best Age for Umrah with Children

  • Infants (0-2 years): Easiest in terms of carrying and mobility, but require many supplies (diapers, milk). Parents can perform rituals relatively easily using comfortable baby carriers.
  • Toddlers (3-6 years): The most challenging age, high energy, needs constant engagement, but perfect for beginning to teach rituals in simplified ways.
  • School-age (7-12 years): Easier in terms of understanding and cooperation. They can actively participate in rituals and understand their meaning. Ideal for a child's first Umrah.
  • Teenagers (13+): Most spiritually beneficial, can perform all rituals independently and grasp deeper lessons.

Planning Ahead: The Key to Success

Good planning is half the journey. At least two weeks before travel:

1. Obtaining Umrah Visas

Every child, including infants, needs their own passport and Umrah permit. Applications can be submitted through the "Eatmarna" platform or a licensed travel agent. Ensure your child's residency permit (Iqama) is valid if they are dependents on a family visa.

2. Hotel Bookings

  • Choose hotels close to the Haram (preferably within 500 meters)
  • Look for family rooms or suites with adequate space
  • Hotels with laundry service to reduce luggage
  • Hotels with child-friendly dining options

3. Packing for Children

  • Ihram clothing for boys, light abayas for girls
  • Comfortable, easy-to-remove shoes
  • Extra diapers and wet wipes (double the usual amount)
  • Reusable water bottle for each child
  • Healthy snacks (biscuits, dried fruits, nuts)
  • Small quiet toys (coloring books, puzzles) for waiting times
  • First-aid kit (fever reducer, burn ointment, bandages, antiseptic)
  • Sun umbrella or cap for sun protection
  • Comfortable baby carrier for infants and toddlers
  • Lightweight, collapsible stroller

Psychological and Educational Preparation

  • Tell children the story of the Kaaba's construction in simple terms
  • Explain Umrah rituals step by step using child-friendly educational videos
  • Teach simple supplications for tawaf and sa'i
  • Agree on behavior rules in the mosque (no running, quiet voices)
  • Promise a reward after completing the rituals
  • Download interactive Umrah apps for children on tablets

Practical Tips During Rituals

During Tawaf

  • Choose non-peak times (after Fajr or after Isha prayers)
  • Use a baby carrier instead of a stroller in crowded conditions
  • Make tawaf interactive, count laps with your child, explain each circuit
  • Take breaks between circuits, drink water near the Hijr area
  • Don't force tired children to complete all circuits; you can complete on their behalf
  • In extreme crowds, perform tawaf on upper floors

During Sa'i

  • Sa'i is easier with children as the walkways are wider
  • Use the distance to teach the story of Hajar and Ismail
  • Designate rest points between Safa and Marwah
  • Encourage children to walk in safe sections
  • Use strollers for young children

Halq or Taqsir

  • For boys: taqsir (trimming) is preferable for young children
  • For girls: clip a small amount from the ends of hair
  • Perform this in a quiet area away from crowds

Health Tips

  • Ensure children's vaccinations are up to date (especially meningitis)
  • Keep children hydrated, especially in summer months
  • Use masks in crowded areas
  • Wash hands frequently or use hand sanitizer
  • Monitor for heat stress signs (headache, dizziness, fatigue)
  • Rest at the hotel if children show signs of exhaustion

Sample 5-Day Family Umrah Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival at Jeddah Airport, transfer to Makkah, hotel check-in, rest and Umrah preparation.

Day 2: Ihram from hotel, Umrah performance in the morning (after Fajr), breakfast at hotel, rest, visit the Clock Tower and shopping area.

Day 3: Fajr at the Haram, morning rest, visit Old Makkah markets, Asr at the Haram, Maghrib and Isha.

Day 4: Travel to Madinah (Haramain train), arrival, prayer at Masjid an-Nabawi, visit the Rawdah.

Day 5: Madinah tour (date farms, Madinah Museum), farewell prayers, departure preparation.

Why Umrah with Children is Worth the Effort

Performing Umrah with children is more than a religious journey, it's a golden opportunity to strengthen family bonds and instill Islamic values in young hearts. Key benefits include:

  • Faith-based upbringing: Seeing the Kaaba and Masjid an-Nabawi firsthand leaves a lasting impression on a child's heart
  • Learning by doing: Children learn rituals best through practice and observation rather than theory
  • Family memories: The journey creates lasting collective memories that strengthen family bonds
  • Islamic identity: Children feel belonging to the global Muslim community when seeing Muslims from around the world united in one place
  • Social skills: Interacting with children from different cultures develops communication and coexistence skills
  • Patience and resilience: The journey teaches patience and enduring difficulty for the sake of worship

Travel Preparation Tips

Traveling with children requires careful planning, especially for an Umrah trip lasting 5-14 days:

Choosing Travel Time

The best times for Umrah with children are periods with mild weather and fewer crowds. Shawwal and Muharram after Hajj season are ideal, with moderate temperatures and smaller crowds. Avoid peak summer (June-August) when temperatures in Makkah and Madinah exceed 45°C.

Flight Bookings

  • Choose direct flights when possible to avoid transit hassles
  • Book seats near restrooms (front of the plane)
  • Travel at times matching children's sleep schedules
  • Inform the airline about children in advance for boarding priority

Travel Health Insurance

Travel health insurance is essential for families. Ensure coverage includes emergency cases in Saudi Arabia, including injuries and sudden illnesses. Some insurers offer Umrah-specific coverage with telemedicine consultations.

Ihram for Children

  • Boys: Wear the two-piece white ihram garment (izar and rida), same as adults
  • Girls: Wear modest clothing covering the entire body (abaya or long dress), with hair covered for girls above the age of discernment
  • Infants: No special clothing required, just clean, modest attire
  • Intention: The guardian says: "O Allah, I intend Umrah for my child; if anything prevents us, then his release is where You have restrained him"
  • Prohibitions: Young children are not obligated, but it's recommended to accustom them to ihram etiquette gradually

Dealing with Common Challenges

Fatigue: Children tire faster than adults. Schedule rest between rituals, and don't try to accomplish everything in one day. Spirituality comes from tranquility, not exhaustion.

Hunger and thirst: Carry snacks and sufficient water. Food and drinks are available from shops near the Haram. Hotels in the central area have restaurants with child-friendly meals.

Crowds: During peak times, use the upper floors of the Saudi expansion for tawaf, or wait until crowds thin. Remember that Umrah isn't limited to tawaf and sa'i, optional prayers and dhikr can be performed anytime.

Boredom: Children get bored easily. Bring small toys, coloring books, and tablets with Islamic educational apps. Make the journey as enjoyable as it is spiritual.

Illness: Wash children's hands frequently and use masks in crowded areas. If a child falls ill during the trip, pause rituals and consult a doctor. Health comes before completing rituals under any circumstance.

Official Resources for Umrah Planning

  • Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah: Official website and "Eatmarna" and "Tawakkalna" apps
  • General Authority for the Care of the Two Holy Mosques: Visiting hours and services
  • Presidency of Religious Affairs of the Two Holy Mosques: Fatwas and religious guidance
  • "Nusuk" app: Official platform for booking Umrah and visit appointments

FAQs about Umrah with Kids

Do infants need an Umrah permit?

Yes, every person performing Umrah needs a permit, including infants. Parents can apply through the Eatmarna app.

Is it allowed to carry children during tawaf?

Yes, it is permissible and common. A comfortable baby carrier is recommended to keep hands free for supplication.

What is the best time for Umrah with children?

Outside peak seasons. Shawwal, Muharram, and Safar are suitable, as is Rajab. Avoid Ramadan with young children due to extreme crowds.

Can I take a stroller into the Haram?

Yes, but it may be difficult in heavy crowds. Baby carriers are preferred for infants and very young children.

What if my child cries during tawaf?

This is normal and expected. Take a break outside the tawaf area, calm your child, then continue. For infants, feed or change them before starting tawaf.
