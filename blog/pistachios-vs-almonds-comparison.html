Pistachios vs Almonds: Which Provides More Protein and Satiety?

Health / Nutrition · June 2026 · Comparison

Pistachios and almonds are two of the most popular nuts in the Gulf region, often enjoyed as snacks or added to traditional dishes. Both are nutritious, but they have distinct nutritional profiles that make each better suited for different goals.

Protein Content

Almonds provide 21 grams of protein per 100 grams, while pistachios provide 20 grams — essentially identical. Both are excellent plant-based protein sources. However, pistachios contain all nine essential amino acids, making them a complete protein — rare for a nut.

Calories and Fat

Almonds have 579 calories per 100 grams with 50g of fat (mostly monounsaturated). Pistachios have 560 calories with 45g of fat. The fat composition is similar, with both being heart-healthy. Pistachios have a slight edge in fiber (10g vs 12g per 100g), with almonds leading slightly.

Satiety and Portion Control

Pistachios have a unique advantage: they come in shells. The act of shelling each nut slows consumption by approximately 40%, and the visual cue of empty shells helps with portion awareness. Studies show that people eating in-shell pistachios consume 40% fewer calories than those eating shelled nuts. Almonds are more convenient for on-the-go snacking but easier to overeat.

The Verdict

Both are excellent choices. Choose pistachios when you want built-in portion control and a complete protein source. Choose almonds for convenience and slightly more fiber.
