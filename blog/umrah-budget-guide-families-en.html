Umrah Budget Guide for Families: Plan Your Trip

2026-06-08 8 min read

Introduction

Performing Umrah as a family is one of the most rewarding spiritual experiences. But without proper budgeting, the costs can quickly spiral beyond what you planned. From visas and flights to hotels and daily expenses, a family Umrah trip requires careful financial planning to ensure you can focus on worship rather than worrying about money.

This guide breaks down every major expense category for a family of four traveling from Saudi Arabia or abroad, with realistic ranges and practical money-saving tips.

Estimated Family Umrah Budget

A 10-day Umrah trip for a family of 4 typically costs SAR 8,000-15,000 for domestic travelers (within Saudi) and SAR 15,000-30,000 for international travelers, depending on season and comfort level.

Key Insights — Citation-Ready

Islamic financial obligations are often misunderstood and under-calculated by Gulf families. Zakat is not merely a charitable donation — it is a compulsory act of worship with precise calculation rules that vary by asset type, holding period, and applicable nisab threshold. For 2025, the nisab threshold based on silver is approximately 2,975 SAR or 2,910 AED. Many families overpay or underpay simply because they do not track their zakat-eligible assets systematically throughout the year.

Visa Costs

Umrah visas are now easier to obtain than ever. Here are the current costs for different nationalities:

  • Gulf residents (non-Saudi): SAR 200-300 per person through authorized agents
  • International travelers: Fees vary by country. Some nationalities receive visa-free or visa-on-arrival access.
  • Insurance: Mandatory health insurance is included in most visa packages (SAR 50-100 per person)

Flights and Transportation

If you are traveling from outside Saudi Arabia, flights are likely your biggest expense. For families inside Saudi, transportation costs are much lower but still significant:

  • Domestic flights (within Saudi): SAR 300-800 per person round trip depending on city of origin and booking time
  • International flights: SAR 1,500-4,000 per person round trip from most countries
  • Local transport in Makkah/Madinah: SAR 200-500 for taxis, ride-sharing, and shuttle buses over 10 days
  • Train (Haramain Express): SAR 150-250 per person between Makkah and Madinah

Hotel Accommodation

Hotels near the Haram are premium-priced, especially during peak seasons (Ramadan, Hajj, school holidays). Here is what you can expect:

  • Budget hotels (1-2 km from Haram): SAR 150-300 per night
  • Mid-range hotels (500m-1 km): SAR 300-600 per night
  • Luxury hotels (walking distance): SAR 600-1,500+ per night
  • Family rooms or suites: Expect to pay 30-50% more than standard rooms

Tip: Book 3-6 months in advance for the best rates. Consider hotels slightly farther from the Haram with shuttle service for significant savings. Once at your hotel, use our Qibla direction finder to locate the prayer direction from your room.

Food and Meals

Food costs in Makkah and Madinah range from budget-friendly to expensive restaurant dining:

  • Self-catering (apartment with kitchen): SAR 50-70 per person per day for groceries
  • Budget restaurants: SAR 15-25 per meal
  • Mid-range restaurants: SAR 40-70 per meal
  • Hotel restaurants: SAR 80-150 per meal

Many hotels offer breakfast included. Making use of this and eating one main meal out per day can significantly reduce food costs.

Daily Transportation and Activities

Beyond the main travel costs, budget for daily expenses:

  • Zamzam water and souvenirs: SAR 200-500
  • Taxi/ride-share within cities: SAR 30-80 per trip
  • Phone/SIM card (if traveling internationally): SAR 50-150
  • Laundry services: SAR 30-60 per load

Money-Saving Tips for Family Umrah

  • Travel during off-peak seasons: December-February and after Ramadan offer lower prices and less crowding
  • Book packages: Umrah packages often bundle visa, flights, and hotels at a discount
  • Use public transport: The Haramain Express train and shuttle buses are much cheaper than taxis
  • Stay in an apartment: Apartment hotels with kitchenettes save on both accommodation and food costs
  • Share transportation: Coordinate with other family groups to split taxi costs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Umrah cost for a family of 4?
A 10-day Umrah trip for a family of 4 costs approximately SAR 7,500-20,000 depending on season, accommodation standards, and origin city. Domestic travelers pay less.

What is the cheapest time to perform Umrah?
The cheapest time is during off-peak months like January-February and September-October. Avoid Ramadan, school holidays, and Hajj season for the lowest prices.

Do I need to book Umrah packages in advance?
Yes, booking 3-6 months in advance secures better rates and availability. Last-minute bookings during peak seasons can cost 30-50% more.

How much should I budget for hotels in Makkah?
Budget SAR 150-300 per night for basic hotels, SAR 300-600 for mid-range, and SAR 600+ for luxury. Proximity to the Haram significantly affects price.

What is the best way to save money on Umrah?
Travel off-peak, book package deals, stay in apartments with kitchenettes, use public transport, and book flights early. These strategies can save 30-40% overall.
