Umrah Budget Guide for Families: Plan Your Trip

2026-06-08 8 min read

Umm Khaled's Story: A Family Umrah That Changed Everything

"I used to think Umrah with young children was impossible because of the cost." That is how Umm Khaled, a 38-year-old mother of three from Dammam, describes her experience two years ago. She wanted to perform Umrah with her family of five, but the budget was the biggest obstacle. Her husband is a government employee with a moderate salary, and three children meant flights, hotels, and meals for five people. "We started saving eight months in advance. Every month we put aside 1,500 SAR in a special Umrah envelope, and we never touched that money except for absolute emergencies. Our strict limit was 12,000 SAR maximum." The surprise was that Umm Khaled and her family not only stayed within their budget but saved an additional 1,500 SAR thanks to careful planning. They traveled in February, which is off-peak season, booked a hotel one kilometer from the Haram with a free shuttle service, and used the Haramain Express train instead of taxis to move between Makkah and Madinah. "It was the best trip of our lives," Umm Khaled says. "The children loved riding the train, and we focused on worship with peace of mind because we knew exactly how much we had spent and how much remained. The difference between planning and not planning is at least 5,000 SAR in our experience." Umm Khaled's story is not an exception, but a model that any Gulf family can achieve with proper financial planning and early saving.

Umrah Cost Overview for Families

Umrah is a deeply spiritual journey, but its costs vary significantly based on several factors: travel timing, departure city, accommodation level, and trip duration. For a family of four traveling from within Saudi Arabia, the total cost of a 10-day trip ranges from 7,500 to 15,000 SAR for a budget plan, rising to 15,000 to 20,000 SAR for a mid-range plan, and potentially exceeding 25,000 SAR for a luxury plan. For families coming from outside the Kingdom, costs increase by 50 to 70 percent due to international flights, bringing the total budget to between 15,000 and 30,000 SAR depending on the country of origin and booking time. The Ministry of Hajj and Umrah provides updated information on regulations and costs through its official portal. The ministry also offers smart applications that simplify Umrah procedures and reduce the need for intermediaries. The single most important factor in determining your budget is the season: peak seasons such as Ramadan and school holidays double hotel and flight prices, while months like January, February, September, and November offer prices up to 40 percent lower. Choosing the right travel time may be the most important financial decision you make before your family Umrah, and it alone can reduce the total cost by one-third or more.

Visas and Insurance: The First Step

Umrah visas are now easier to obtain than ever thanks to digital advances introduced by the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. For Gulf residents who are not Saudi nationals, the visa cost through authorized agents ranges from 200 to 300 SAR per person. For international travelers, fees vary by nationality, with some nationalities receiving visa-free or visa-on-arrival access. Mandatory health insurance is included in most visa packages at a cost of 50 to 100 SAR per person. We recommend dealing with agents authorized by the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah to ensure you receive a valid visa without delays. You can check updated visa requirements on the Visit Saudi website, which provides comprehensive information for travelers from different nationalities, including required documents, processing times, and fee structures.

Flights and Transportation

If you are traveling from outside Saudi Arabia, flights represent the largest item in your Umrah budget. For families within Saudi, transportation costs are lower but still significant. Domestic flights between Saudi cities and Makkah or Madinah cost 300 to 800 SAR per person round trip depending on the departure city and booking time. International flights from most Arab and Islamic countries range from 1,500 to 4,000 SAR per person round trip. Saudi Arabian Airlines offers special Umrah deals on its official website, especially for early bookings and group travel. For local transportation in Makkah and Madinah, budget 200 to 500 SAR for taxis, ride-sharing apps, and shuttle buses over 10 days. The Haramain Express train is an excellent option for travel between Makkah and Madinah, costing 150 to 250 SAR per person, and it is faster and cheaper than private taxis. The train stations are modern, air-conditioned, and equipped with facilities for families with children.

Hotel Accommodation: Choosing the Best for Your Family

Accommodation is the second largest item in your budget after flights, with prices varying significantly based on proximity to the Haram and the season. Budget hotels located 1 to 2 kilometers from the Haram range from 150 to 300 SAR per night for a standard room. Mid-range hotels at a distance of 500 meters to 1 kilometer cost 300 to 600 SAR per night. Luxury hotels within walking distance of the Haram exceed 600 SAR and can reach 1,500 SAR per night or more during peak seasons like Ramadan, when some hotels overlooking the Haram may charge up to 5,000 SAR per night. For families, we recommend apartment hotels with small kitchens, which provide flexibility for meal preparation and significant savings on food costs. Booking family rooms or suites increases the cost by 30 to 50 percent compared to standard rooms, but provides the privacy and space that families need. Book 3 to 6 months in advance for the best rates, and consider hotels slightly farther from the Haram with shuttle service to save up to 40 percent on accommodation costs. A smart alternative is staying near the Abraj Al Bait towers or hotels near Ajyad, where free or low-cost shuttle services to the Haram are available.

Food and Meals: Smart Shopping Saves Significantly

Food costs in Makkah and Madinah range from very budget-friendly to expensive hotel dining. The best option for families is apartment hotels with kitchenettes, where you can prepare breakfast and dinner at a cost of 50 to 70 SAR per person per day for groceries. Budget restaurants offer meals from 15 to 25 SAR, while mid-range restaurants range from 40 to 70 SAR per meal. Hotel restaurants may charge 80 to 150 SAR per meal. Many hotels offer complimentary breakfast, and making use of this service while eating only one main meal out per day can reduce food costs by up to 50 percent. We recommend purchasing water and snacks from major supermarkets outside the central Haram area, where prices are noticeably lower than the small shops surrounding the Grand Mosque.

Smart Strategies to Save Money on Family Umrah

Here are seven practical strategies that thousands of families have used and found effective. First, travel during off-peak seasons: January, February, September, and November offer prices up to 40 percent lower compared to Ramadan and school holidays. Second, book an all-inclusive Umrah package: comprehensive packages bundle visa, flights, hotels, and transportation at a discount of 15 to 25 percent compared to separate bookings. Many travel agencies offer family-specific packages that include extra amenities like children's activities and family-oriented hotel rooms. Third, use public transportation: the Haramain Express train and shuttle buses between hotels and the Haram are much cheaper than private taxis. Fourth, choose apartment hotels with kitchenettes to save on both accommodation and food costs, as a family can prepare breakfast and dinner for as little as 50 SAR per person per day. Fifth, share transportation with other families staying at the same hotel to split taxi costs for group trips, such as a visit to Madinah or the holy sites. Sixth, purchase Zamzam water and souvenirs from major supermarkets like Carrefour or Panda outside the central area, where prices are 30 to 50 percent lower than shops surrounding the Haram. Seventh, use available planning tools such as the Travel Budget Calculator to set a precise budget before traveling, and the Currency Converter to check updated exchange rates before dealing with exchange offices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Umrah cost for a family of 4?

A 10-day Umrah trip for a family of four costs approximately 7,500 to 20,000 SAR depending on the season, accommodation standard, and departure city. Travelers from within Saudi Arabia pay significantly less than international travelers. For an accurate estimate, use the Travel Budget Calculator with your specific trip details.

What is the cheapest time to perform Umrah?

The best time for lower costs is outside peak seasons, specifically January, February, September, and November. During these months, hotel and flight prices are up to 40 percent lower compared to Ramadan and school holidays. The crowds are also smaller, making it easier to perform the rituals with children.

Do I need to book Umrah packages in advance?

Yes, booking 3 to 6 months in advance secures better rates and more options. Last-minute bookings during peak seasons can cost 20 to 30 percent more and limit hotel choices near the Haram. All-inclusive packages typically save 15 to 25 percent compared to booking everything separately.

Can families perform Umrah with young children?

Yes, thousands of families perform Umrah with children every year. The key is good planning: choose a hotel close to the Haram to minimize walking with children, travel in cooler months, maintain flexible prayer and rest times, and carry water and snacks. Consult a scholar about specific rulings regarding children performing the rituals.

What is the best way to save money on family Umrah?

The best strategies are: travel during off-peak seasons, book an all-inclusive package, choose an apartment hotel with a kitchenette, use the Haramain Express train and shuttle buses, and book hotels and flights early. Applying these strategies together can reduce the total cost by 30 to 40 percent.

Umrah with your family is a priceless spiritual experience, and good financial planning is the key to enjoying it without worry. Start saving early, set a realistic budget based on the information in this guide, and use available tools like the Travel Budget Calculator to fine-tune your numbers. Remember that the goal is not the lowest possible cost, but the best value that meets your family's needs and allows you to focus on the essence of the journey: worship and drawing closer to Allah. Plan ahead, book early, and your family Umrah will be spiritually fulfilling and financially manageable.
