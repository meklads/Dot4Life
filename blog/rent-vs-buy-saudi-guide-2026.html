    Conclusion

    The rent-versus-buy decision is not about which is universally better. It is about which is better for you, right now. Calculate your numbers honestly, account for all costs, consider your timeline, and factor in your family's needs. If buying is the right choice, the REDF programs and competitive mortgage market make it more accessible than ever in Saudi Arabia. If renting is the right choice, do it intentionally: save the difference between rent and what a mortgage would cost, and build your down payment for when the time is right.
