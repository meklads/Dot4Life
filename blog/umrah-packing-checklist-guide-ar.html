Introduction

العمرة رحلة روحانية تحتاج تحضيراً دقيقاً. قائمة التعبئة الصحيحة تضمن راحة بالك وتفرغك للعبادة دون قلق على النواقص. هذا الدليل يغطي كل ما تحتاجه للعمرة مع العائلة.

Ihram and Prayer Essentials

For men, the ihram consists of two white seamless cloths. Bring at least two sets — one for wearing and one spare. For women, there is no special ihram garment; modest clothing that covers the full body (excluding hands and face) is worn. Essentials include:

  • 2-3 sets of ihram cloth for men (pre-washed and ironed)
  • 1-2 pairs of comfortable sandals or flip-flops (men in ihram cannot wear stitched footwear)
  • Modest, loose-fitting abayas or dresses for women (lightweight fabrics recommended)
  • Prayer mat (lightweight, travel-sized)
  • Quran and dua book (or use phone apps)
  • Tasb

Footwear

You will walk extensively — often 5-15 km daily within the Haram area. Good footwear is essential:

  • Comfortable walking sandals or sports sandals (primary footwear)
  • Slip-on shoes for entering the Haram (you remove shoes at the door)
  • Light socks (3-4 pairs, moisture-wicking)
  • For men in ihram: open-toed sandals that do not cover the ankle bone
