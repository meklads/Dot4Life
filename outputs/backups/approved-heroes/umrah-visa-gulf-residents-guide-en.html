Umrah Visa for Gulf Residents: Complete Guide 2026

2026-06-236 min read

The answer depends on your country of residence. Saudi Arabia residents don't need a separate Umrah visa if their iqama (residency) is valid - they apply through the "Eatmarna" or "Tawakkalna" apps for a direct permit. Residents of other Gulf countries (UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain) can easily obtain an electronic Umrah visa through the official "Nusuk" platform.

How to Apply for an Umrah Visa

Applying for an Umrah visa has become significantly easier with digital platforms. Follow these steps:

First, visit the Nusuk website or download the Nusuk app. Create an account using your email and verify your identity. Select "Umrah Visa" from the services menu. Fill in your personal details, passport information, and residency details. Upload scanned copies of your passport and residency permit (Iqama). Choose your preferred visa type based on your needs and budget. Pay the fee online using a credit card. Once approved, you will receive your e-visa via email. Print it and carry it with you during travel.

You can apply for an Umrah visa online through the Nusuk platform (nusuk.sa) run by the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. The platform provides services for pilgrims from all over the world.

Nusuk (nusuk.sa) is the official Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah platform. Steps: Create account, enter personal data, upload passport and residency copies, choose Umrah date. Visas are typically issued within 24-48 hours.

  • Check passport and iqama validity before applying
  • Apply at least 2 weeks before travel
  • Book hotels close to the Haram
  • Get required vaccinations (meningitis is mandatory)
  • Keep digital and printed copies of all documents
  • If rejected, reapply after 30 days
  • Use the visa within its validity period

Umrah visa fees: Basic visa fee: 200-300 SAR per adult. Mandatory health insurance: 50-100 SAR. Processing fee: 20-30 SAR. Total per person: approximately 270-430 SAR. Children (2-18 years): reduced fee. Children under 2: may be exempt. Payment via credit card or Mada. Keep payment receipt.

Each GCC country has specific procedures. UAE residents can apply via Nusuk directly or through Umrah offices in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Kuwaiti residents apply through licensed travel agencies or Nusuk. Qatari residents can apply via Nusuk or Doha travel agencies. Omani residents apply through Nusuk or agencies in Muscat and Salalah. Bahraini residents have similar options via Nusuk or Manama travel agencies.

Required Documents and Fees

The standard documents required include a valid passport with at least 6 months validity, a copy of your Gulf residency permit (Iqama) valid for at least 3 months, a passport-sized photograph with white background, and proof of accommodation in Saudi Arabia. Some applicants may also need to provide a travel itinerary and return ticket. Additional documents may be requested depending on your nationality and the visa type you are applying for. It is always best to prepare all documents in advance to avoid delays in processing.

According to the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah (haj.gov.sa), visa fees vary depending on the applicant nationality and visa type. Electronic visas for residents range from 300 to 500 SAR.

Fees through Nusuk range from 200-500 SAR per person, including mandatory health insurance. Children under 18 receive a reduced fee. Infants under 2 may be exempted in some cases.

Umrah visa allows entry to Saudi Arabia for up to 30 days from issuance. It permits Umrah performance, visiting the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, and tourism in other Saudi cities.

Some cases may require: certificate of infectious disease clearance (health sector workers), confirmed hotel bookings for all stay days, return flight ticket with booking proof, bank statement (rare cases), guardian approval for minors traveling without parents. Prepare these in advance to avoid processing delays.

If your visa is rejected, check the reason on the platform. Common causes: incomplete or unclear documents, passport validity under 6 months, iqama validity under 3 months, data entry errors. Correct the issue and reapply after 30 days. If rejection persists, contact Nusuk support at 800-443-0000 (within Saudi) or via email. Alternative: apply through an authorized travel agent or licensed tawafa offices.

Having a previous Umrah visa and using it properly increases your approval chances for future applications, as the platform tracks your application history and visa compliance.

Visa Validity and Stay Period

The Umrah visa is typically valid for 30 days from the date of issue, allowing you to stay in Saudi Arabia for up to 30 days. Some visa types allow multiple entries during the validity period. It is important to check the specific conditions of your visa before traveling. Overstaying the visa period can result in fines and future travel restrictions.

Saudi residents use "Eatmarna" or "Tawakkalna" apps. Steps: Open app, select "Perform Umrah", choose time slot (morning/evening), confirm booking. Permit is free and issued instantly. Each person can get one permit every 10 days approximately.

Types of Visas for Gulf Residents

There are several visa options available for Gulf residents wishing to perform Umrah. The most common is the electronic Umrah visa (e-visa), which can be obtained online through the Nusuk platform. There is also the visa on arrival option, available for residents of certain countries who hold valid residency in a Gulf state. Additionally, family visit visas and tourist visas may also permit Umrah, though conditions vary. Each visa type has different requirements, fees, and validity periods, so it is essential to choose the one that best suits your situation.

Nusuk (nusuk.sa) is the official Ministry of Hajj and Umrah platform. Steps: First, create an account with your email and phone. Second, select "Perform Umrah" from the main menu. Third, add personal data: full name as in passport, passport number, expiry date, nationality, iqama number and expiry. Fourth, upload required documents (passport copy, iqama copy, photo). Fifth, choose your preferred Umrah date. Sixth, select health insurance package. Seventh, complete online payment. You'll receive your e-visa within 24-48 hours.

Important Tips for Residents

The Saudi Embassy (saudiembassy.net) recommends ensuring your passport is valid for at least 6 months before applying for an Umrah visa.

In addition to Umrah visas, several visa types are available for Gulf residents. The Saudi tourist visa allows tourism and Umrah simultaneously, valid for 90 days renewable. Family visit visas allow residents to host or visit family. Transit visas are available for travelers passing through. Choose the right type: for Umrah only, use the Umrah visa; for tourism, the tourist visa offers longer stays and greater flexibility.

Multiple-entry Umrah visas are now available for some nationalities, allowing multiple entries within 90 days - perfect for Gulf residents in nearby countries who wish to visit frequently.

If you prefer not to apply yourself, you can use travel agencies authorized by the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. These agencies handle the entire process for an additional fee of 50-150 SAR. Authorized agencies can also provide complete Umrah packages including visas, hotel bookings, flight tickets, and internal transportation. Verify agency authorization through the ministry's website before engaging their services.

Beware of unlicensed agencies offering suspiciously cheap visas. These agencies may use tourist visas instead of Umrah visas, causing problems at entry. Only deal with licensed, trusted agencies. Verify licenses through the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah website or call 800-443-0000.

Some Gulf resident nationalities qualify for Umrah visa on arrival at Saudi airports. This service is available to holders of valid Gulf residencies from specific nationalities. The on-arrival visa costs the same as the e-visa but saves advance application time. However, we recommend applying in advance via Nusuk to avoid airport queues and ensure visa approval before travel. Some nationalities don't qualify for visa on arrival and must apply in advance. Check the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs website before traveling to see if your nationality qualifies.

Remember to check the official Saudi government websites for the most up-to-date information, as visa policies can change. Planning ahead and preparing all documents will ensure a smooth and spiritually fulfilling Umrah journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I perform Umrah on a family visit visa?

Yes, family visit visas to Saudi Arabia generally permit Umrah, but verify the visa doesn't specifically prohibit it.

How many Umrahs per year?

Saudi residents can get a permit every 10 days. Gulf residents can apply for a new visa each time.

Do children need separate visas?

Yes, every person needs their own visa or permit, including children and infants.

What if my visa is rejected?

Reapply after 30 days, ensuring all requirements and documents are correctly submitted.
