A Saudi Mother's Journey: Balancing Work and Parenting

June 2026 · 8 min read

Introduction

In today's world, working mothers face enormous challenges balancing career demands with childcare responsibilities. In Saudi Arabia, where development accelerates and women's work opportunities expand, inspiring success stories emerge of mothers who overcame these challenges with intelligence and creativity. This article shares one Saudi mother's story and how she faced these challenges with practical solutions.

Key Challenges

  1. Time constraints: Juggling long work hours with household duties and childcare.
  2. Psychological pressure: Feelings of guilt toward children or shortcomings at work.
  3. Lack of support: Sometimes lacking sufficient support from spouse or extended family.
  4. Finding reliable childcare: Finding nurseries or nannies that align with family values and work requirements.

Practical Solutions from Real Experience

The Saudi mother shares her experience and offers solutions that helped her:

  1. Advance planning and time management: Creating a detailed daily and weekly schedule dividing work hours, household tasks, and children's activities. Using time management apps and digital planners.
  2. Distributing roles and seeking support: Discussing with spouse to fairly divide household tasks and childcare responsibilities. Asking grandparents or relatives for help when needed.
  3. Leveraging work flexibility: Seeking jobs with flexible hours or remote work options. Negotiating with employer for mother-friendly arrangements.
  4. Choosing appropriate childcare: Carefully researching nurseries with good reputation matching educational standards. Hiring qualified nannies after verifying their background and experience.
  5. Allocating time for self-care: Importance of dedicating time for rest and relaxation to avoid burnout. Pursuing hobbies and activities that help recharge energy.

Conclusion

This Saudi mother's story is a testament to the power of determination and perseverance. With good planning, appropriate support, and flexibility, working mothers in Saudi Arabia can achieve balance between their career ambitions and family happiness, becoming role models for future generations.
