🕋 Daily Morning & Evening Adhkar — Gulf Family Guide

🕋 Islamic📅 June 15, 2026⏱ 6 min read

Daily remembrances (Adhkar) are a fortress for the Muslim family. Teaching children to remember Allah at the start and end of each day instills faith in their hearts and makes remembrance a natural habit.

Morning Adhkar

• "O Allah, by You we enter the morning, by You we enter the evening, by You we live, by You we die, and to You is the resurrection."
• "We have entered the morning upon the fitrah of Islam and the word of sincerity..."
• Ayat al-Kursi
• Surah al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq, an-Nas (3 times each)

Evening Adhkar

• "O Allah, by You we enter the evening, by You we enter the morning..."
• Evening duas for protection
• Surah al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq, an-Nas (3 times each)

After Prayer Adhkar

• SubhanAllah (33 times)
• Alhamdulillah (33 times)
• Allahu Akbar (33 times)
• La ilaha illallah, wahdahu la sharika lah...

🕌 Prayer times: Use Prayer Times to know prayer times in your city.

Sleeping Adhkar

• "In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live."
• Ayat al-Kursi
• Surah al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq, an-Nas
• "O Allah, protect me from Your punishment on the Day You resurrect Your servants."

Teaching Children Adhkar

• Lead by example — children imitate.
• Make it a family activity.
• Use colorful charts.
• Reward consistency.
• Link adhkar to specific times (after Fajr, before bed).
