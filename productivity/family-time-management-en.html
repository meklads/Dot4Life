📋 Family Time Management — A Balanced Daily Schedule for Gulf Families

📋 Productivity & Family📅 June 15, 2026⏱ 7 min read

In our busy world, family time management is a necessity, not a luxury. How do we balance work, school, worship, leisure, and sleep? The answer is a balanced daily schedule.

Family Organization Tips

• Quick weekly family meeting (Sunday) to plan.
• Daily screen-free time.
• Distribute chores — kids can help.
• Don't over-schedule — leave room for spontaneity.
• Early sleep is the productivity secret.

Reducing Screen Time

• Max 2 hours daily (TV + phone).
• Use Screen Time apps.
• Offer alternatives: board games, reading, outdoor play.
